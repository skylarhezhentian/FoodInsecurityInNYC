# Working-paper archive

This folder preserves the June 2026 working paper recovered with the research
code. It documents the motivation, model formulation, earlier experiments, and
proposed extensions that preceded the symposium poster.

- `research_paper.md` is the readable manuscript.
- `research_paper.tex` is the recovered typeset source, with its figure files.
- `build_tex.py` is the original Markdown-to-LaTeX conversion helper. It requires
  Pandoc and overwrites the adjacent TeX file, so work on a copy if using it.
- `manifest.json` records the unchanged source and asset hashes.

These are **working drafts, not a published paper or the current results**. The
manuscript includes provisional reference entries, a literature feature table
whose claims were not fully checked, and proposed recipient-capacity/recourse
extensions that were not evaluated. Earlier single-run results differ from the
later five-replicate poster study. The historical solver limitations also apply.

Use the [poster evidence guide](../../poster/README.md) to trace what reached the
poster, and the [current methods](../../docs/methodology.md) for the corrected
model. The manuscript is preserved as research history; its draft claims and
unfinished references are not promoted to new validated findings.
